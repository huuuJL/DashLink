REUSED UNCHANGED RECEIVER VALIDATION

LIMITS
- Locally signed (ad hoc); not Developer ID-signed or Apple-notarized.
- Experimental accessory authentication; not Apple-certified for CarPlay.
- ARM64 only. Live testing used macOS 27.2; macOS 15 compatibility is a build
  target, not a completed minimum-version hardware test.
- No claim of full Siri/call, sleep/wake, roaming, or long-session qualification.
- No independent security audit or broad device compatibility certification.
